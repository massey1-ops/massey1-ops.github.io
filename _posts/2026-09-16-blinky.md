The goal of the Blink If assignment was to turn 4 different lights of the arduino Lilypad on depending on the run time of the program. This included different delay times, both with lights on and off, and various colors.

My Tip: If your arduino is being weird, open device manager on your computer and check the port to manage the connection status.
