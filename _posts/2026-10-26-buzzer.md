In this assignment, I made using the buzzer on the lilypad to "sing" Baa Baa Black Sheep. I found the notes (here)[https://www.davidsides.com/products/baa-baa-black-sheep-easy-piano-sheet-music?srsltid=AYCX9vJln8pXbUp5m59luM7afo4H2KbBejRGqxKXVzADaIQ7icP9sU5V].
I had to find the frequency equlivalences to each note and play them in succession, adding a long delay at the end before the song repeats. 
A tip I would give to my past self would be to make a list/chart of the verses with the notes and frquencies before coding.
