The project combines electrinucs and sewing into a small plushy that includes 3 LEDs, a button, and a switch. When the button and switch are both on, the LEDs light up. 

A tip I found along the way was to cut the extra felt into small pieces to use for stuffing. The excess fills the plushy perfectly.
